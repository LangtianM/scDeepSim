\noindent\textbf{Property definitions.}
\begin{description}
  \item[De novo simulation.] Generates synthetic cells from a learned distribution without requiring a matched observed cell as input at generation time.
  \item[Latent representation.] Learns low-dimensional latent variables that represent major single-cell variations and can be used for downstream generation, or signal extraction.
  \item[Interpretable control.] Exposes control knobs with direct biological or technical meaning, such as batch effect, pseudo-time, or noise level.
  \item[Extrapolative control.] Generates or transforms samples under effect strengths or factor combinations that are not directly observed in training data, including stronger-than-observed batch effect and shifted developmental trajectories.
\end{description}
